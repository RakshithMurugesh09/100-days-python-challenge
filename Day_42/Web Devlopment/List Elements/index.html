Rakshith's Recipe for the Best Cinnamon Rolls

Ingredients

For the Dough:

  • ¾ cup warm milk
  • 2 ¼ teaspoons yeast
  • ¼ cup granulated sugar
  • 1 egg plus 1 egg yolk
  • ¼ cup butter

For the Filling:

  • ⅔ cup dark brown sugar
  • 1 ½ tablespoons ground cinnamon
  • ¼ cup butter

Instructions

  1. Mix the milk with the yeast, sugar, eggs
  2. Add the melted butter and stir until combined.
  3. Gradually add flour and knead the dough until it becomes smooth and elastic.
  4. Place the dough in a greased bowl, cover, and let it rise for about 1 hour or until doubled in size.
  5. In a small bowl, mix the brown sugar and cinnamon for the filling.
  6. Roll the dough out into a large rectangle.
  7. Spread the softened butter over the dough and sprinkle the cinnamon sugar mixture evenly on top.
  8. Roll the dough tightly into a log and cut it into equal-sized rolls.
  9. Place the rolls in a greased baking dish and let them rise for another 30 minutes.
  10. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  11. Bake the cinnamon rolls for 20–25 minutes or until golden brown.
  12. Allow the rolls to cool slightly before serving.
